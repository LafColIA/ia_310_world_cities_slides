The Shangluo Corridor (Shi, 2023)

  • The Shangluo Corridor is a least-cost path through the Qinling
  • The Shangshan-Wuguan route runs from pre-Qin into imperial times
  • 107 stone bi disks at Donglongshan; Indian Ocean cowries at Laoniupo

Endpoints and surveyed corridor as reported. The modelled paths are not drawn: the source gives no route geometry.

Qinling: the mountain range that walls the Guanzhong basin off to the south, crossed only at its passes. Shi modelled least-cost paths from the Longshan centres of Qijiaping and Shimao to the Middle Yangzi at Xiangyang, and the Shangluo Corridor came out cheaper than the Yellow River valley.

09

The Han city was a fortress

  • A 26 km rammed-earth wall enclosing about 34 km², about 2.7 times the area of Easton, PA (Xie, 2024)
  • Twelve gates, each garrisoned; deliberately asymmetrical walls
  • Built on Qin ruins with walls following the Zao River (Steinhardt, 1986)
10

Between the Han and Tang capitals

  • Han Chang'an largely destroyed under Wang Mang before 25 CE (Britannica, 2026)
  • The Eastern Han capital moved east to Luoyang in 25 CE (Britannica, 2026)
  • Western Jin capital 311–316, then Sixteen Kingdoms states, Western Wei and Northern Zhou (Britannica, 2026)
  • The Sui refounded Chang'an in 582 on ground the Han city never held (Chen et al., 2023; Wen, 2024)

The East Market was the base unit (Chen et al., 2023)

  • The East Market at 924,000 m², about 2.1 times Lafayette's main campus, is the modulus
  • 27 urban objects match a multiple of the East Market at 98.5% or better
  • 475 pairwise ratios match a culturally significant value

Map of Chang'an in Tang Dynasty. LackOfInspiration1, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0. Set on white for legibility: the map's labels are black.

13

The ceiling on a city run by writing

  • Without writing, a compact settlement stays near 100 ha, about 2.2 times Lafayette's main campus (Fletcher, 2020)
  • With writing, a compact settlement tops out near 15–30 km², about 1.2–2.4 times the area of Easton, PA (Fletcher, 2020)
  • Chang'an reached 90–100 km², about 7.5 times the area of Easton, PA, and 0.8–1 million people (Fletcher, 2020)
  • The capital absorbed 40% of the annual grain tax (Fletcher, 2020, quoting Wright, 1967)

Compact settlement: one whose residents live at high density on a continuous built-up area, as against a low-density settlement spread thinly over a much larger territory.

Arrival by conquest and exile

  • Nearly 10,000 households settled from 630 after the Turkic defeat (Rong, 2025)
  • The Sogdian state of An submitted about 5,000 troops in 630 (Rong, 2025)
  • Persian royalty took refuge after the Sasanian collapse (Rong, 2025)
  • The Great Mosque of Xi'an was founded in 742 and still stands (Britannica, 2026)

904, and everything after

18

Cut down by an order of magnitude

  • Han Jian dismantled Chang'an in 904; the court went to Luoyang, then Kaifeng (Wen, 2024)
  • The Sui and Tang walled area was 84.1 km², about 6.7 times the area of Easton, PA (Wen, 2024)
  • The Ming walls of 1370–78 enclosed 12–14 km², about the area of Easton, PA (Guo et al., 2026)

Wen (2024); Guo et al. (2026) for the Ming figure.

Ruins became goods (Wen, 2024)

  • Liu Chang collected about a hundred stones from the Tang garden
  • The stones became purchasable commodities in Song markets
  • Ouyang Xiu: Liu Chang's carts "could barely hold" the antiquities

The Tang city as a product (Zhu, 2017)

  • The 2005 Imperial City Restoration Plan budgets 23 billion yuan
  • Daming Palace park demolished about 20,000 houses

What to take away

  • Han Chang'an and Tang Chang'an were almost two different cities (Steinhardt, 1986; Chen et al., 2023)
  • The city was cosmopolitan by conquest as much as by trade (Rong, 2025)
  • Losing capital status remade the city for a thousand years (Wen, 2024; Zhu, 2017)
