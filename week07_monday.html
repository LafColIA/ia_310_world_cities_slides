06

Sourcing Angkor Borei's beads (Carter et al., 2021)

  • Over 1,300 glass and stone beads sourced by composition
  • Sri Lankan glass from Giribawa, garnets from the Garibpet deposit
  • Stronger affinities to inland sites than to contemporary coastal ones
  • Smaller mass-produced beads by 100 to 300 CE, a network scaling up

Oc Eo culture gallery, Museum of Vietnamese History, Hanoi. Gary Todd, CC0. Angkor Borei's own assemblage is not pictured.

07

A monkey as a diplomatic gift (Sugiyama et al., 2022)

  • Oxygen isotopes record a move from a humid to an arid environment
  • Captured before age three, held in captivity over two years
  • A 7.1‰ carbon enrichment toward a maize-heavy diet
  • Exchange predating the documented Entrada by roughly a century

Structure a hinterland cannot explain

12

City size across a whole country (Li et al., 2024)

  • Detrended fluctuation analysis across 37 European countries
  • Statistically significant long-range correlation in how city size is arranged in space
  • Structure that no local provisioning model predicts

Hurst exponent (H): a measure of how city size is arranged along a spatial transect, where 0.5 is no spatial ordering at all.

13

Two arrangements

The two regimes, drawn schematically. Exponents and the one-third span from Li et al. (2024); the circles are not data.

14

Where each arrangement appears (Li et al., 2024)

  • Clustered in Bulgaria, Germany and much of central and northern Europe
  • Alternating in Spain and Finland, the less common of the two
  • Almost every alternating country has an extended coastline, Austria and Slovenia excepted
  • Exponents hold their side of 0.5 from 100 m to 800 m clustering thresholds

16

Faster links sort rather than spread (Qiao & Ascani, 2025)

  • Entering an industry rises 0.3 percentage points per unit of industry complexity
  • Against a baseline entry probability of 3.3%, a 9.1% relative increase
  • Effects accumulating over four years
  • Benefit concentrating in already-developed eastern hub cities

The count that stayed still (Han & Sng, 2026)

  • Conventional accounts hold county-level units at 1,200 to 1,300 for two millennia
  • Population grew across the same span, implying declining administrative reach
  • Skinner proposed 1,180 to 1,385 units for unified dynasties

What made a zhen a county (Han & Sng, 2026)

  • Demarcated boundaries with registered households
  • Centrally appointed officials with judicial and civil authority
  • Tax collection and public services
  • Direct reporting to a supervising prefecture
22

The state placed them at commercial nodes (Han & Sng, 2026)

  • Placement responding to population growth and fiscal opportunity
  • A settlement hierarchy partly an artefact of where officials were installed
  • The hierarchy an output of the network rather than an independent structure to test it against

Failaka: the institutions arrive together (Raja & Sindbæk, 2023)

  • Weight systems, stamp seals and Babylonian cuneiform arriving in combination
  • Ship and sailing technology and token currency alongside
  • Heterogeneity concentrated in a small exchange-oriented settlement

Antwerp's merchant nations (Harreld, 2003)

  • Hanse, English and Portuguese nation houses, legally recognised, 1468 to 1562
  • A purpose-built exchange, the Bourse, built in 1485 and rebuilt much larger in 1532

Pieter van der Borcht, the Antwerp Bourse, etching 1582–1588. University Library of Antwerp, CC0.

30

The Bourse was copied outward (Harreld, 2003)

  • Thomas Gresham modelled London's Royal Exchange on Antwerp's Bourse
  • The Amsterdam exchange drew on both London's and Antwerp's

Pieter van der Borcht, the Antwerp Bourse, etching 1582–1588. University Library of Antwerp, CC0.

31

What the Bourse cost the neighbourhood (Harreld, 2003)

  • Poorer residents pushed to the urban periphery after the 1532 relocation
  • Merchant-driven rents rising around the new exchange
  • Institutional concentration and sharp spatial inequality arriving together
32

The peruleros are the other half (Bonialian, 2023)

  • 24 documented merchant agents moving between Seville, Portobelo, Acapulco, Manila and Canton
  • Potosí silver invested directly in Chinese goods at the Manila pancada
  • An estimated 30 to 50% of the silver reaching China through prohibited channels

The tradings and commerce of this kingdom [Peru] with that [Spain] are being so much reduced that the money is taken to New Spain for use.

— a royal official, quoted in Bonialian, 2023

34

What to take away

  • City sizes correlate across whole countries and faster links sort rather than spread activity
    • Long-range correlation across 37 European countries (Li et al., 2024)
    • Industrial entry concentrating in already-developed eastern hubs (Qiao & Ascani, 2025)
  • Commercial networks and governance network structures often overlap
    • About 1,800 basic administrative units in Song China by 1085, sited at commercial nodes (Han & Sng, 2026)
  • Diasporas emerge from and anchor exchanges
    • Antwerp's nation houses, 1468 to 1562, and a Bourse copied to London and Amsterdam (Harreld, 2003)
